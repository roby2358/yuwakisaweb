# Caravans & Conquest

A resource-and-position strategy game built on the Hex & Counters foundation. Open `index.html` directly in a browser; no server or build step is required.

Cross the frontier, gather timber and ore, fulfill contracts at trading posts, and arrive at the Crown Market with 15 influence. Fighting raiders earns useful coin but raises unrest, so force solves immediate problems while making the campaign more dangerous.

## Controls

- Click the gold caravan (`C`) to show movement and adjacent attack options.
- Click a yellow hex to move. Forests yield timber, quarries yield ore, and gold terrain yields coin once.
- Click an adjacent red-highlighted raider (`R`) to fight it. Force costs 1 provision and 1 ore.
- On a market (`M`) or the Crown Market (`★`), use **Contract** or **Buy Supplies**.
- Press Space or Enter, or click **End Turn**, to consume one provision and resolve raiders.
- Left-click and drag past the click threshold to pan. Escape clears selection.
