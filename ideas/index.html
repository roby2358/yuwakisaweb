Game Ideas

Prototypes and experiments, mostly hex-and-counter games. Each one runs in the browser.

  • Dustrunneralienbadlands

    Stranded in an alien badlands with a gravbike and 40 credits, you harvest what the waste grows and haul it home past bandits to afford a 4,000-credit ticket offworld.

  • Auguraugur

    As the village augur, your visions are always true but never complete. Each day you choose between divining more of the future and acting on it, while the knowing slowly drives you mad.

  • Laurelsbest

    A hero whose renown melts every dawn must keep feeding their legend through acquisition, prestige and destruction. Rest on your laurels and be forgotten.

  • Biome Warsbiomewars

    Living biomes on an alien planet grind against each other hex by hex, pushed by settlements and blights. You are one small hero thriving in the cracks.

  • Connect Webconnectweb

    A generative sketch: random dots connected to every other dot, and wherever two lines cross, the longer one is removed.

  • The Waking Islediscoverescape

    Explore a fog-shrouded island for relic tombs. Every relic you take wakes hunters, and the only way out is back to the boat.

  • DIVE — the Chroma Deepdive

    Prospect a colorful alien ocean rift: your sub has unlimited range, but your dive suit has very little air.

  • RADIO FREE SECTOR 9dystop00

    A 1987-floppy-style insurrection game. Plant pirate transmitters to wake a sedated city block by block in chain reactions, while the regime's Sweepers hunt your five named rigs.

  • En Garde! — The Season in Parisengarde

    A swashbuckling social campaign: climb the status ladder of 17th-century Paris through clubs, regiments, gambling, mistresses and duels.

  • Flow Fieldfield

    A visual toy: a procedural vector field from Perlin noise, with particles flowing through it.

  • Founders' Roadfoundersroad

    Lead a crew of workers into a procedurally generated wilderness, building sawmills, stoneworks, mines and roads while beasts press in.

  • Gemracegemrace

    An expedition and return journey: collect three Sunstones and get home, while monsters chase you and temporary rule effects reshape each run.

  • CHARTER — a game of governancegovern

    As Regent of the river-city of Lexden for 24 seasons, you govern by writing edicts and rulings, and clerks will carry them out exactly as written.

  • Healerheal

    You are the healer for a party of AI adventurers. You can't control them, only keep them alive with a baroque, tiered list of support skills.

  • Hex & Countershexandcounter

    The reusable base platform for the hex games: a movement puzzle about route-finding across a generated map on a tight MP budget.

  • Laurelslaurels

    A persistent game of acquisition, prestige and destruction, in which your renown decays daily unless you keep earning it.

  • The Great-Souled Samlordoflight

    A single-player strategy game adapted from Roger Zelazny's Lord of Light.

  • Monster Gladiatorsmglad

    A hex-and-sprite remake of a 1992 Turbo C++ game. Buy monsters, fight arena rounds, and promote your stable up the leagues.

  • PDF Explorerpdfexplorer

    An interactive explorer for probability distribution functions.

  • PowerRangepowerrange

    City-states wage war with massive cannon platforms and shields. Underneath, it's an economics simulation.

  • Racersracers

    Slot cars racing around a 3D track, rendered from scratch.

  • Reclaimerreclaimer

    Your colony ship crash-landed on a blighted world. With a hull of frozen colonists and one trusty blaster, the only way off is through.

  • RIVERWORLD — Up the Riverriverworld

    A turn-based expedition up Philip José Farmer's million-mile river, waking on its bank as whoever you were in life.

  • The Night of the Simoorsimoor

    A bawdy PG-13 game of mistaken identity at a masked desert revel: find the Veiled Sovereign in a crowd of identically veiled figures.

  • Embergradesomegame

    A wildfire is spreading across the island. Command three firefighters and keep every village from burning for 15 days.

  • Signal Citysomegame2

    An outnumbered superhero team triages crises across a city while a hidden Mastermind's plan ticks toward zero.

  • Supersuper

    A superhero with a randomly rolled set of powers takes on a campaign of events across perilous maps.

  • Solar Corsairswashbuckler

    A space-corsair reskin of the hex movement game: plot a course across a generated map to reach the target on the far side.

  • Thing Factorythingthatdoesathing

    Build a contraption one Thing at a time while it runs. Belts, springs and pullers shove and drag crates toward the target, and wandering gremlins ride along, sometimes into the sea.

  • Thredthred

    A Forth-like language driven by a stack and command words.

  • Thrivethrive

    Marooned in the lawless shantyport of an alien wasteland, you hustle for enough credits to buy a one-way ticket through the Gate.

  • TIGER! TIGER!tigertiger

    A turn-based revenge hunt adapted from Alfred Bester's The Stars My Destination.

  • Total Wartotalwar

    Grand strategy on Avalon Hill's Third Reich skeleton, in a WWII-shaped world where dieselpunk, fantasy and retro sci-fi armies fight side by side.

  • Caravans & Conquesttradewars

    Gather timber and ore, fill contracts at trading posts, and reach the Crown Market with 15 influence. Fighting raiders pays, but it raises unrest.

  • Void Seal — The Unravelingvoidseal

    Rifts pour creatures of anti-reality into the world and turn the land to Void. A warband of sorcerers and technologists must seal them.

  • Weavers of Worlds: The Loomweavers

    An ensemble of cosmic artisans weaves new worlds on the Loom of Creation before entropy tears the fabric apart.

  • Wilds of Mariguezwildsofmariguez

    Two unlikely companions push into a hostile wilderness, where every treasure they claim reshapes the journey and draws deadlier attention.

  • Hex & Counters: Living Worldworld

    A world with no end: villages grow, prosperity draws raiders, and you earn prestige defending what grows.
