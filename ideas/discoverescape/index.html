Turn 1 Relics 0/5 Hunters 0

The isle is silent — for now. Explore the fog, find the relic tombs (✦), and carry your plunder back to the boat (⛵) to escape.

Every relic you take wakes hunters — and the more you carry, the farther they smell you. Run far enough and they lose the scent. Hills see farther; standing stones (▲) reveal much. At turn 60, night falls and the isle fully wakes.

Escape with one relic and you win. Escape with all five and you're a legend.
