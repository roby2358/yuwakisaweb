Thing Factory

Crates come out of the source. Build a contraption that gets them to the ★ target: 10, 20, 50, 100, and on and on. Every Thing does one thing, every turn. Gremlins wander in and ride along.

Draw Things from your hand of three and lay them on the board. Each one you place ticks the world once, so build where it counts.
