Thing Factory: reconsider how big crates (D-34) drive advancement. Right now the big-crate chance is m/(m+4) per spawn and a big crate is worth 10, so milestone gaps stay roughly flat (bot, small board, to the 5th milestone: turns 49/76/104/139/199 with big crates vs 49/82/180/342/662 without). That may flatten the endless milestone ladder (10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000) too much, or turn late play into "wait for big crates". Options to weigh: a gentler curve or cap on the chance; scaling the value with the milestone instead of a fixed 10; making big crates heavier (e.g. Springs can't throw them, Belts move them every other tick) so they're worth more but cost more to route; tying them to the crate cap (count as more than 1). Playtest first, then pick with cc-sim-to numbers.
