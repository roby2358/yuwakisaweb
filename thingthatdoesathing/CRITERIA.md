# Thing Factory — Criteria

How we know the build is right. Each row has a stable ID that is never reused. Rules are in
ONTOLOGY.md, cited by name. Constants are cited by name, never by value.

**Instrument** is the row's status. It takes one of four forms: `automated`, `manual — <what
a human checks>`, `not yet covered`, or `retired`. An `automated` R-row owns `test/R-n/`. Each
test there asserts that one rule, with its cases commented in the test itself.

**Test command:** `just cc-test`, which runs `node test/run.js` (add `-v` for every check). It runs each `test/R-n/`
file, the integration tests (`smoke.js`: random legal play with invariant checks;
`uiboot.js`: the real UI booted on a stub DOM, early mouse/key/resize/timer events on the intro before any board exists, then played to the first milestone by simulated clicks, plus Go/Pause and the color picker), and
`test/coverage.js`. **Tuning harness:**
`node test/sim.js <games> <size> <milestones>` (a greedy builder bot; not pass/fail): `just cc-sim N SIZE` plays to the first milestone, `just cc-sim-to N SIZE M` on to the Mth.

## R — Rules (automated)

| ID | Given | Expect | Instrument |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-1 | `newGame(seed, size)`, every size | `cols × rows` hexes of `MAP_SIZES[size]`, each `(col,row)` once, keyed `"q,r"` with `q = col - floor(row/2)`; `state.size` recorded | automated |
| R-2 | any generated map, every size | `isEdge` exactly on the outer ring, and every edge hex is `WATER` | automated |
| R-3 | any generated map | inner hexes ranked by elevation split at `WATER_PCT`/`MOUNTAIN_PCT`; ICE only in the plains band, `round(ICE_FRAC × inner)` of it | automated |
| R-4 | any generated map | the first source in the leftmost placement slice, its target in the rightmost (Place source and target rule) | automated |
| R-5 | many seeds at every size | a land path from the first source to its target | automated |
| R-6 | `newGame(seed, size)` | one source and one target sharing a `CRATE_COLORS` color, and one crate of that color on the source; gremlin count in `gremlinDice(size)`d`GREMLIN_DICE_SIDES` (one die per `GREMLIN_HEXES_PER_DIE` hexes of area, rounded, at least 1) on distinct passable inner hexes off the source and target, each colored; no Things; `HAND_SIZE` cards, all from the starting `UNLOCKS` step (`unlocks` 1, R-49); counters reset; unique ids | automated |
| R-7 | a ground step (a Belt's push) | blocked (piece stays) into off-map, mountain (a Spring's flight passes over, R-53), Wall, or a piece that stays (a Cannon's shot passes over all of these for its first 4 hexes, R-54; one that leaves that hop doesn't block, R-55); accepted onto plains, ice, a bridge, a Slide, or a mover | automated |
| R-8 | `legalPlacements(kind)` | exactly the Placement legality rule: not a target (a Thing there doesn't matter, R-47), right ground, no piece under a blocking kind, and no blocking kind on a source | automated |
| R-9 | `place(slot, q, r, facing, color)` | legal: Thing appended in `color` (fixture facing null), slot redrawn, hand stays `HAND_SIZE`, one tick. Illegal (slot, facing, hex, or a color not in `colorChoices`, R-50): `{ok:false}`, state and `Rando` unchanged | automated |
| R-10 | `rotate(q, r)` | on a Belt, Bridge, Spring, Rocket, Cannon, Pusher, or Puller: facing turns clockwise and nothing else changes (no tick: `events` empty, turn, pieces, hand untouched). On a fixture or empty hex: `{ok:false}`, unchanged | automated |
| R-11 | `wait()` | one tick, hand and Things untouched | automated |
| R-12 | a piece on a mover during a tick | pushed `THINGS[kind].push` hexes in the facing, stopping before a blocked hex (a piece there on slick ground is bumped, and the mover follows it, R-51) | automated |
| R-13 | a tick | each piece gets at most one motion, claimed as the tick starts (a line of belts advances a crate one hex per tick; a carried gremlin doesn't also wander) | automated |
| R-14 | a piece entering bare ICE | keeps going the same way, free, until non-ice or blocked; ice under a Thing doesn't slide (unless that Thing slides, R-48); a flying push (Spring, Rocket) counts ice under its flight; every landing (Spring, Rocket, Cannon; full range or short) on bare ice skids on (on the ground, D-37) | automated |
| R-15 | a piece entering water | removed if no Thing is there (crate: `lost` + its `value`); a bridge takes it, and carries it on next tick (R-46); a flying push (Spring, Rocket) passes over inland water and sinks only if it comes down on it, but the outer sea always takes it | automated |
| R-16 | a piece entering a target | a crate of its color is removed and `delivered` + its `value` (1; a big crate 10, R-52); a gremlin that comes onto any target dies (`splat`, `by` null, after its `move`; `exGremlins` +1, a new one climbs out), but not when a flight carries it over | automated |
| R-17 | a spinner during a tick | the Spinner was replaced by the Puller (R-28) | retired |
| R-18 | gremlins in a tick | each gremlin no Thing claimed steps to a random enterable (or crate-holding) non-water neighbor, picked from the board as the tick starts, or stays; boxed in → stays; never walks into open water; pieces never share a hex | automated |
| R-19 | ticks | `spawnTimer` counts up; at `SPAWN_INTERVAL` the tick is a spawn chance and the timer resets; on a chance, if the crates on the board (a big crate counts one) number fewer than `crateCap(size)` (max(1, round(`CRATE_CAP_PER_ROOT` × √area)): small 8, medium 12, large 18, huge 36) plus one per milestone reached, the tick rolls an open color, and if that color's source is empty a crate (or big crate, R-52) of that color spawns there; at the cap, or with that source occupied, nothing spawns until the next chance | automated |
| R-20 | (a final win at `DELIVER_GOAL`) | — | retired |
| R-21 | two games, same seed | identical terrain, source, target, pieces, hand; different seeds differ | automated |
| R-22 | two engines, same seed and commands | identical state and events after every command; the engine never calls `Math.random` | automated |
| R-23 | `GEN_MAX_ATTEMPTS` pathless maps, every size | falls back to that size's `knownGoodSeed`, records it, equals `newGame(knownGoodSeed, size)`; that seed has a path on attempt 1 | automated |
| R-24 | the engine-side modules | load and play with no DOM, canvas, or `GameDisplayArtifacts`, and never reference them | automated |
| R-25 | any command | a boolean `ok`; `newGame` without a non-negative integer seed or a `MAP_SIZES` key is `ok:false`; `ok:true` from place/rotate/remove/discard/wait carries `events[]`; `ok:false` carries nothing else | automated |
| R-26 | a tick's events | `move` paths list every hex entered, one per hop from the move's `hop` (`over` 0 but for a shot's cleared hexes), and end where the piece is or vanished; `sink`/`deliver`/`splat` follow their `move` and carry the hop they happened on (`blast`'s and `remove`'s at hop 0); `spawn` reports the new crate; `open` and then `unlock` follow their `milestone`; replaying events reproduces every position | automated |
| R-27 | many draws | each card's kind comes from `THINGS` by weight (only known kinds, shares near weight / total over the kinds in the deck); the deck is the unlocked kinds (R-49), so a new game deals only `UNLOCKS[0]`; with one color open the `colored` kinds are out of the deck, with two or more every unlocked kind is in; a card is just its kind, with no color (a `colored` kind's color is picked at place, R-50) | automated |
| R-28 | a Puller during a tick | the piece on the far hex (two ahead) as the tick starts is pulled one step back into the near hex by the ordinary ground step (blocked → stays, water → sinks, ice → slides onto the Puller); pieces on the Puller or the near hex are left alone; a piece carried onto the far hex during the tick isn't pulled until the next | automated |
| R-29 | `remove(q, r)` | the Thing at (q,r) is gone, one tick, hand unchanged; a piece left on open water by a removed Bridge sinks (lost if cargo) with its `sink` event first. Empty hex: `{ok:false}`, unchanged | automated |
| R-30 | milestones | `nextMilestone` gives 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000, … with no last one; the tick whose deliveries reach one emits one `milestone{count}` (deliveries of every color count); other ticks emit none; commands keep working past any milestone | automated |
| R-31 | a gremlin sinks or splats | at the end of that command a new gremlin (new id, palette color) appears on a random shore hex (land next to an edge hex, free, not source or target), its `spawn` after the `sink`/`splat` and before any `milestone` (a piece sunk by `remove` before the tick included); a sunk crate brings nothing back; a full shore brings nothing back | automated |
| R-32 | a Rocket during a tick | the piece on it flies up to `THINGS.ROCKET.push` hexes in the facing, or until stopped: before a blocker (a piece on slick ground is bumped and the flight ends behind it, R-51), sunk in the outer sea (crate: `lost` +1), delivered crossing its color's target; it flies over inland open water, Bridges and ice (every hex counts, no slide mid-air), and sinks if it comes down on water; coming down on bare ice or a Slide, at full range or short, it skids on, on the ground (D-37): a skid into open water sinks it, onto its color's target delivers it, stops at a Wall or another color's target, and bumps a piece on ice (line +2 from hop 9, ends 1 past the hit hex); a short landing on ice skids too (blocked mid-flight by a Wall: the Wall stops the skid; down short of another color's target, which stops the skid); the one exception: a flight that splats a gremlin stops on its hex, no skid; a launched gremlin splats where it lands; one `move` for the whole flight and skid; Belts still sink in inland water | automated |
| R-33 | a Rocket's flight and gremlins | a flier stopped by a gremlin splats it (`splat{id, by}`) and lands on its hex; a launched gremlin splats where it lands (`splat`, `by` null) unless it sank; splats aren't `lost`; other pushers never splat | automated |
| R-34 | `discard(slot)` | the slot gets a fresh draw, one tick, Things and other cards untouched. Bad slot: `{ok:false}`, unchanged | automated |
| R-35 | a gremlin comes onto a Trap | it dies (`splat`, `by` null, after its `move`) whether it wandered, was pushed, slid on ice, or came down from a flight (a Spring's skid included); a flight passing over a Trap doesn't trigger it; a Trap built under a gremlin doesn't kill it; crates cross Traps safely | automated |
| R-36 | gremlins gone | `exGremlins` goes up by one for every gremlin sunk, splatted by a Rocket, killed on a target, or sunk by removing its Bridge, but not one a Trap caught (R-42); crates never count; a new game starts it at 0 | automated |
| R-37 | a gremlin's own step into a crate | the crate is wrecked (`splat{id: crate, by: gremlin}` after the gremlin's `move`), `lost` + its `value`, nothing re-emerges, and the gremlin takes the hex; a gremlin pushed, pulled or flung into a crate is only blocked | automated |
| R-38 | a milestone | opens the next color until `MAP_SIZES[size].colors`: a color not open yet, from an existing source (`ENHANCE_CHANCE`; both outcomes happen) or a new source in the left slice, with its target in the right slice on a free hex its source has a land path to; `open{color, source, target}` right after the `milestone`; nothing past the cap or with no free hex (the next milestone tries again); small never opens one | automated |
| R-39 | a crate meets a target | its own color's target delivers it (walking, pushed, or crossing it in flight) and counts it in that target's `delivered` and the board's; another color's target is solid on the ground; a flight passes over it but drops one hex short rather than come down on it | automated |
| R-40 | a colored Pusher or Puller | acts only on crates of its color and on every gremlin, leaving other crates where they are; a white one acts on every piece | automated |
| R-41 | a Pusher during a tick | pushes the piece on the hex it faces `THINGS.PUSHER.shove` hexes on in its facing, through the same `step` (blocked, sinks, slides on ice); ignores a piece on its own hex; turns like any mover | automated |
| R-42 | a gremlin caught by a Trap | `trapped` goes up by one (and `exGremlins` doesn't), whether it was pushed or came down from a flight; its `splat` carries `trap: true`, every other splat `trap: false`; a new game starts it at 0 | automated |
| R-43 | the order Things act in a tick | Pushers and Pullers act before every other Thing, each group in placement order, so one built beside a running Belt line catches the crate in front of it whichever was placed first | automated |
| R-44 | `blast(q, r)` | on a crate: the crate is gone, `lost` + its `value`, its `splat` (`by` null) leads the events, Things untouched, one tick. On a gremlin or an empty hex: `{ok:false}`, state and `Rando` unchanged | automated |
| R-45 | ticks with no card used | after `IDLE_REDRAW_TICKS` ticks in a row of `wait`, `remove` or `blast`, one random slot is redrawn (`redraw{slot}`) and the count starts over; `place` and `discard` reset it; `rotate` doesn't tick or count | automated |
| R-46 | a Bridge during a tick | pushes the piece on it `THINGS.BRIDGE.push` hexes in its facing, on the ground, through the same ground step as a Belt: onto land or the next Bridge, blocked by a Wall or a piece, sunk in open water (crate: `lost` +1); a piece that came onto it this tick waits for the next (R-13); carries gremlins too | automated |
| R-47 | `place` on a hex holding a Thing | the old Thing is gone and the new one stands there, last in placement order, as one command and one tick (Thing count unchanged); it acts this tick on a piece standing there; same kind over itself is legal; the hex is judged as if bare: a land kind can't replace a Bridge on water, a Wall can't go where a piece stands | automated |
| R-48 | a Slide (`THINGS.slides`) | a piece that comes onto it on the ground keeps going the same way, free, through the same slick test as bare ICE, until ground that isn't slick or a blocker; built on water (`on: 'both'`) the piece slides over it unsunk and sinks in open water beyond; a Slide on ice still slides; a Spring or Rocket that comes down on one skids on (D-37), and Slides under a flight don't count; a piece standing on one stays put; no facing (`rotate` illegal); removing a water Slide sinks the piece on it | automated |
| R-49 | milestones and the deck | a new game opens the first `UNLOCKS` step only; each milestone opens the next step (`unlock{kinds}` after that `milestone` and its `open`), and its kinds join the deck; on a one-color board a step of only `colored` kinds is passed over and the unlock names the next; crossing several milestones at once unlocks one step per milestone, in order; past the last step nothing unlocks; the cards in hand stay as they are | automated |
| R-50 | a Thing's color at `place` | `colorChoices(kind)` is the open colors (in opening order) for a `colored` kind, `[WHITE]` for any other; `place` with one of them builds the Thing in that color, which it keeps for good (`rotate` doesn't change it) and acts by (R-40); any other color (`WHITE` or a closed color for a `colored` kind, a crate color or none for a plain kind) → `{ok:false}`, state and `Rando` unchanged | automated |
| R-51 | a mover (any Thing's push, a Spring's or Rocket's flight, a Cannon's ground step, a skid or ice slide, a gremlin) stopped by a piece that has had no motion this tick standing on slick ground (bare ICE, a Slide) | bump (D-33, D-38): from the contact hop the line from that piece on (it and the motionless pieces right behind it; only the bumped hex need be slick) advances 2 hexes together, each a ground step (no slide after; sinks in water, `lost` += value; delivered on its color's target; a gremlin dies on a Trap; a Wall, mountain or the piece ahead stops it, so a blocked line compresses); the bumper waits the contact hop and follows from the next as far as the bumped piece went, ending right behind it, and the bumped hex is left empty; a line that can't move leaves the bumper where it was stopped (a flight comes down there); the bump ends the bumper's motion, a flight's included; line `move`s start on the contact hop, after the bumper's `move`; a Rocket still splats a gremlin and a gremlin's own step still wrecks a crate first; a piece that already had a motion this tick isn't bumped and ends a line; a gremlin with nowhere to wander has none and can be bumped; a piece on plains is only a blocker | automated |
| R-52 | a spawn, a big crate's delivery or loss | a spawn is a `bigcrate` with `bigCrateChance(milestonesReached(delivered))` = m / (m + `BIG_CRATE_HALF`) (0 before the first milestone, with no `Rando` roll; rising toward 1, never 1), else a crate; it counts as one toward the crate cap; delivered, it adds `PIECES.bigcrate.value` to `delivered` and its target's count; sunk, wrecked or blasted, it adds that value to `lost`; Things move it like a crate (a Rocket doesn't splat it); a delivery that crosses several milestones at once emits each one's `milestone`, `open` and `unlock` in order | automated |
| R-53 | a Spring's throw (`overMountains`) meeting mountains | it passes over them: over one or two mountains it lands on the third hex; it can't come down on a mountain, so a throw that ends over one lands on the last hex short of it that it flew over (mountain 3rd: on the 2nd; 2nd and 3rd: on the 1st; all three: no throw, no `move`); past mountains it still skids on ice and sinks in a lake; stepped back off a mountain onto ice it skids on too, and the mountain stops the skid (D-37); a Wall or a piece on plains still stops it, and stopped over a mountain it comes down short of it; a Rocket (no `overMountains`) is still stopped by a mountain | automated |
| R-54 | a Cannon (`clears` 4, `push` 5) during a tick | the piece on it passes over its first 4 hexes touching nothing: water, a mountain, a Wall, a piece (a gremlin there isn't splatted), its color's target (not delivered), a Trap (a shot gremlin isn't caught); one `move` lists all the hexes; the 5th is an ordinary ground step (hop 5): open water or the outer sea sinks it, its color's target delivers it, a Trap kills a shot gremlin, bare ice or a Slide slides it on, once (the ground slide is its skid, D-37: one `move`, each hex once; a slide into open water sinks it, onto a Trap kills a gremlin), a piece on ice there is bumped (line +2 from hop 5, the shot ends 1 past the hit hex); blocked there (a Wall, a mountain, another color's target) it comes down on the 4th hex (a Bridge, bare ice or a Slide there takes it; on ice or a Slide it skids on, D-37, and what blocked the 5th stops it, each hex once in the `move`; its color's target there delivers it), but if the 4th is open water, a mountain, a Wall, a piece or another color's target it doesn't fire (no `move`); a path that leaves the map within the 4 doesn't fire; a piece passed over still gets its own push that tick; the Cannon is the last `UNLOCKS` step, after the Rocket (R-49) | automated |
| R-55 | a tick (lockstep, D-38) | every moving piece advances one hex per hop, all together; a hex whose occupant leaves on a hop is open that hop, so a packed train on a Belt line advances as one whatever order the Belts were placed in | automated |
| R-56 | a flight or skid reaching a hex | it meets what's there at that hop: a Rocket's crate that comes down on ice skids on through a hex a Belt emptied on an earlier hop of the same tick (not stuck on the ice); a crate that stays still stops it | automated |
| R-57 | a cycle of 3 or more pieces each moving into the next one's hex | all move: a packed ring of Belts (6, or 3) turns one hex per tick | automated |
| R-58 | two pieces each moving into the other's hex | both blocked and stay (a head-on swap fails), and a piece pushing into the stuck pair is blocked too | automated |
| R-59 | two pieces after one hex on one hop | the one claimed by the Thing earlier in the tick's order wins (Pushers and Pullers first, then placement order, D-24); the other is blocked and stops for the rest of the tick, even if the hex frees up on a later hop | automated |
| R-60 | two airborne pieces entering one hex on one hop, or swapping hexes | both crash: gone, each `lost` += value, a `splat` each naming the other (`by`) on that hop; a flight meeting a piece on the ground only comes down short and the piece stays; a flight and a ground mover after one hex tie by rank, no crash; a Cannon's cleared hexes pass over a flight unseen | automated |
| R-61 | any tick (random play with every kind of Thing, crowded) | replaying its events hop by hop (`test/helpers/hops.js`) never puts two pieces on one hex (a shot's `over` hexes aside); `smoke.js` checks the same on every command, and `uiboot.js` checks the UI's animation tracks never draw two pieces on one hex at a hop | automated |
