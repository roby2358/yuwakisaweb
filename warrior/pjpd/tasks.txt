Add a Vigor skill school — the "HP-as-mana" school. Vigor is the fourth stat with no offensive identity (currently just an HP sponge). Give "health monsters" oomph by making HP a resource you spend and convert, not just a buffer you hoard.

TWO-AXIS MODEL (the key framing; weapon-leverage and source-of-power are SEPARATE questions):
- Axis 1 — weapon class (the end-of-line glyph, the `weaponClass` field): does the skill layer the equipped weapon's affix/damage? melee 🗡️ / ranged 🏹 / direct ☄️ ("fires from nothing," no weapon leveraged).
- Axis 2 — school / resource (cost line + factors text): where the power comes from and what it costs — Might/Reflex/Warding/Vigor, paid in MP/AE/HP. The scaling stat already shows in factors ("+Vig" etc.); resource cost shows in the cost line.
- They compose freely: a skill can be weaponClass 'melee' (🗡️) AND cost HP / scale Vigor. That's exactly how a Vigor skill that flows through a weapon is expressed.

NO new glyph (decided): do NOT add a heart or any "source of power" glyph. The scaling stat is already named in the factors text, and source-of-power isn't single-valued (e.g. void_strike = weapon+Might+Warding) so one icon can't represent it. Vigor-ness is conveyed by HP cost (cost line) + "+Vig" (factors). [Deferred idea, NOT now: color the factor stat tokens — Mgt red / Ref green / War purple / Vig crimson — for at-a-glance build scanning; handles multi-stat skills with no new symbols.]

CONCEPT / power budget: each school has a signature resource — weapon skills spend MP, warding spends AE, Vigor spends HP. Vigor is a resource/amplifier school layered over your weapon: Might/Reflex/Warding answer "where does damage come from," Vigor answers "what will you pay to amplify it / how do you survive." Cross-pollinates with Might (bruiser builds: Might weapon + Vigor) — explicitly desired. No artificial HP-spend cap: the hostile board (Unraveller, 45-Might roamers) is the governor — dumping your whole bar into one hit is a real, usually-suicidal, occasionally-run-saving option (windfall/comedy). Glass cannons already do poorly here, so giving the survival stat offense rehabilitates a forced tax into a real build.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE — brute force = restrained variety: option count scales inversely with bluntness. Melee has many riders (cleave/stun/burn/shred/execute), ranged has projectile geometry (penetrate/ricochet/volley), warding has area/chains. Vigor's quadrant is DISPLACEMENT & IMPACT and does NO tricks — no penetrate, ricochet, shred, or burn (those belong to other schools). Keep it to ~5 blunt, highly-readable skills.

SKILLS:
- Buildup strike (keystone) — SAME-TURN: spend HP for bonus damage flowing through the equipped weapon (weapon + Vigor, paid in HP); weaponClass = whatever weapon is held. This is dimensional_rend generalized/promoted — dimensional_rend is already this exact template (weapon + Vigor drawn from HP), so build on it. Deferred-charge/wind-up idea was REJECTED: same-turn is more readable, needs no state, truer to brute force.
- Warp Shield — convert from AE cost to HP cost ("rechannel life force into a ward"); HP-fueled chance to block all damage for a turn. weaponClass 'direct'. Defensive pole of the HP economy.
- Overwhelm — guaranteed stagger/stun paid in HP (reliability, NOT a chance — the brute version of stun_blow). Brute force is deterministic.
- 2-hex Knockback — single big shove (vs shockwave's 1-hex AoE nudge); the drama/board-control verb (knock enemies into maws / off positioning / into each other).
- Lifedrain AoE — adopt existing lifedrain_blast (Vigor AoE + heal per hit) as the refill that closes the HP loop.
- (Maybe) Lay Bare — channel HP to strip a % of target's defense, deterministic, hefty all-in HP cost. CAVEAT: %-def-strip is "shred," which is melee's verb (sundering_blow) and collides with Vigor's no-tricks identity. If added, model as a Might/Vigor CROSSOVER: weaponClass 'melee' (layers the weapon) + HP cost — "sundering_blow's all-in big brother." Don't bolt on a fail-chance; the hefty HP cost vs the lethal board is the gamble. Decide whether it's worth the slight identity dilution before adding.
