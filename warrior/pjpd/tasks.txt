Add more ranged enemies and put a visual marker on the enemy token so the player can tell at a glance which counters are ranged threats.

(1) Roster expansion — current ranged enemy lineup is sparse. Add ranged variants across the tier curve so ranged threat shows up in early, mid, and late game, not just one or two creature types. Suggested directions, mapping to existing creature themes:
  - Early-tier: skirmishers / archers (low damage, range 2-3)
  - Mid-tier: spellslingers / hex-hurlers (medium damage, range 3-4, possibly chaos-flavored)
  - Late/guardian-tier: a ranged guardian or two so endgame fights aren't all melee bruisers
  - Garrison-defender ranged variant for variety on garrison kill events

Each new enemy gets a creatureDef in config.js (or wherever ENEMY_DEFS lives) with appropriate damage, range, HP, AI. Ranged enemy AI should already exist for the current ranged ones — reuse, don't fork.

(2) Visual marker on the token — small overlay so ranged enemies are identifiable without hovering. Options to pick from:
  - A small bow/arrow glyph in a corner of the token
  - A colored ring or dot on the token border
  - A subtle range-indicator color shift on the enemy's name/HP tag
Whichever option, keep it readable in fog (revealed-but-not-visible state) and at zoom — don't crowd the existing token sprite. Implementation likely lives in the token-draw pass in index.js or in SpriteSheet.drawCell consumers; either overlay-draw after the token, or add a small accent sprite to the sheet.

Tactical justification: ranged enemies meaningfully change approach planning — the player decides whether to close, take cover behind terrain (forest/hill defense bonuses already exist), or out-range them. Without a visual marker the player has to memorize creature types or hover everything, which is tedious. The marker is small UX work for a clear gameplay payoff.

Tuning: don't overpopulate ranged on a single map — they're a counter to slow players and can feel oppressive in clusters. Aim for ~20-30% of spawns being ranged at the appropriate tier, not 50%+.
